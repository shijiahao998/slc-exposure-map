About this map

The static basemap is always enabled. Click facilities, parcels, routes or stops to inspect their recorded attributes. With Radius Analysis enabled, the same click also updates the selected area. Points take priority over routes, then parcels.

Radius counts use straight-line, great-circle distance to mapped facility points. One mile is 1,609.344 metres. The radius is not clipped to the county; the facility dataset covers Salt Lake County only.

Co-located fire and EMS records within 30 m share one response-location marker. Fire and EMS role counts can overlap. Sensitive/critical points at identical rounded coordinates share one marker.

Buildings use archived OSM heights where available; floor counts use 3 m per level. Optional tan extrusions are a 6 m illustration where height is unknown.

Live Transit shows current UTA vehicle positions through the configured Vercel proxy while enabled, refreshing about every 30 seconds. Bus and train icons identify vehicles, with blue for bus, green for TRAX/rail and purple for FrontRunner using existing static route IDs; unmatched routes use ?. Positions older than three minutes are omitted. Live Traffic displays TomTom traffic tiles through the proxy and refreshes every 10 minutes while enabled. This map does not calculate population, land-use composition or exposure scores.
