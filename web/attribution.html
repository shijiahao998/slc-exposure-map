Sources & attribution

This static map contains archived display data for Salt Lake County. Source names do not imply endorsement. Mapped locations do not establish present operation, staffing, capacity, public access or response times.

OpenStreetMap

© OpenStreetMap contributors, under ODbL 1.0. Roads, water, places, sensitive/critical facilities and buildings derive from the Geofabrik Utah extract, snapshot September 8, 2026. Geometry was selected, clipped, simplified or tiled for display. Polygon facilities use representative points. Sensitive points sharing coordinates rounded to five decimal places share a marker. Unused building-tile attributes were removed; existing classified parcel tiles are retained. Geometry is unchanged.

Building heights use archived OSM tags: reported height where present or floor count × 3 m. Unknown heights stay unknown; optional 6 m extrusions are illustrative. Heights are not independently surveyed.

UGRC / Salt Lake County

Data Sources: UGRC SGID (data modified), Salt Lake County assessor records. The source policy specifies CC BY 4.0 unless dataset metadata says otherwise. Parcel snapshot: March 26, 2026. Parcels are shown as outlines and Land Use colors from assessor property classes, which are proxies rather than zoning. Existing classified parcel display tiles are reused unchanged; county boundary acquisition date is unknown.

Fire Stations: catalog December 2025 (service description separately references September 2022). EMS Facilities: historical June 2013 catalog, UGRC / DHHS / DEM. County-filtered public facility attributes are retained. Fire/EMS records within 30 m are combined into response locations with both roles preserved. This is a display deduplication rule, not proof of a shared organization.

Utah Transit Authority static GTFS

Source: Utah Transit Authority, archived scheduled GTFS feed 2026 AUG_20261008, advertised service dates August 16–December 5, 2026. Prepared route shapes and stop/station points are reused unchanged from the local map. Route shapes are clipped/simplified for display; multiple archived variants may be shown. Data retain UTA terms of use. No live vehicle locations, arrivals or current service status are provided.

Stored parcel population attributes

Source: U.S. Census Bureau ACS 2020–2024 five-year total population, B01001_E001. The existing residential parcel-area allocation is displayed as a stored attribute only; this static page does not recalculate parcel population or estimate population within a radius. Source block-group margins of error are not parcel-level uncertainty.

USGS / USDA imagery

Optional online raster tiles from USGS The National Map / USDA historical aerial imagery. Acquisition dates vary by location. Imagery is requested directly only when enabled; no USGS imagery archive or access key is packaged.

Hexagon high-resolution imagery

© 2026 HxGN Content Program, Hexagon. Downloaded through UGRC Discover. The Aerial imagery control includes all nine downloaded high-resolution images at zoom 12 and above, with online USGS imagery retained underneath and outside coverage. Their filenames specify a contiguous 3 × 3 standard XYZ Web Mercator grid: x=6202–6204, y=12313–12315, z=15. Each 8192 × 8192 image is subdivided into 512-pixel JPEG tiles at zoom 19 with a zoom-18 overview and smaller WebP overviews for zooms 12–17. Neighboring images share the same exact XYZ grid; low-zoom overviews combine their coverage with transparent padding outside it. No manual fitting or reprojection is applied. The filename date 10-14-2026 is retained as a download identifier, not asserted as the flight date. All original images are unchanged.
